SCENE 5 — WORKFLOW BUILDER
Duration: 2:50–3:40
Visual: Automations canvas — zoomed large (setup collapsed to corner)
================================================================================

VOICEOVER:

Next: the Workflow Builder.

This is where you decide what happens after the AI extracts the data.

A typical flow looks like this:
  A Gmail trigger starts the run.
  AI extraction structures the message.
  Optional validation checks the fields.
  Google Sheets is updated.
  Your team can get a notification.

When a new message arrives, the workflow runs on its own — no copy-paste,
no waiting for someone to update a sheet.

Start simple, or grow into multi-step automations — without writing code.

VISUAL ACTIONS:
  2:50–3:05  Open workflow; zoom so nodes fill the canvas
  3:05–3:20  Trace path with cursor: Trigger → Extract → Sheet → (notify)
  3:20–3:35  Click Test run or show a recent successful run
  3:35–3:40  Brief highlight of Live status

      ↓
  AI Extraction
      ↓
  Validation (optional)
      ↓
  Google Sheet Update
      ↓
  Team Notification (optional)
